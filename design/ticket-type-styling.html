Ticket type styling

Ten ways to tell a normal ticket, a command ticket and an orchestrator ticket apart on the board. Every card uses the real card styles; each option shows the ticket at rest and while it is running. A to E are quiet and in the app's own style; F to I are louder, recognizable shapes; J is the mix of G and F.

Already in use on the card, so the options stay clear of it: blue / violet left rail = build / plan mode coloured left stripe = mark red outline = error amber dashed outline = blocker on hover sky ring = sidebar hover
